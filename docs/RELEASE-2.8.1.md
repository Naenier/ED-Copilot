# ED Copilot 2.8.1

- The overlay uses compact cards, aligned quantities and two equal side bands. Flight, trade, carrier, mining, exobiology and engineering panels show the information needed for the active task.
- Colony supplies group ship deliveries, local carrier unloading and purchases. Unloading uses fresh, accessible stock on a carrier confirmed in the colony system. Each list shows how many additional rows are available when space runs out.
