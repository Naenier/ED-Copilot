# ED Copilot 2.8.14

- Carrier routes show current fuel when a matching live observation is available. Planned jumps burn fuel from the tank, with separate amounts for transfers from storage and tritium sourced along the route.
- The carrier overlay shows bank balance, weekly upkeep and occupied capacity alongside tritium stocks. Journal events update the tank and warehouse as you travel, transfer cargo or donate fuel.
- Colonisation adds facility effects and system-development projections, with separate charts for observed population and political changes.
- Exploration adds expeditions, discovery rules, personal records, surveys, and a surface radar in the desktop app and overlay.
- Save and compare ship builds, plan visits to engineers and material traders, and follow ship routes with optional next-system clipboard copying.
- Review installed modules in **Engineering → Current ship**. See completed and partial upgrades, compatible blueprints, workshop unlock steps and material estimates.
- Configure overlay and Windows voice notifications, or share selected read-only panels with a paired second screen on the local network.
- Compare mining runs and manage shared ship and carrier cargo reservations across colony construction projects. The updater adds package provenance checks and recovery improvements.
