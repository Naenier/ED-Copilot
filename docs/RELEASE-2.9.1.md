# ED Copilot 2.9.1

## Ready-made ship builds

- Open **Engineering → Ship builder → Ready builds** to browse 91 fits covering all 49 ships. Filter by ship or role, then open a fit as a draft. Adapted builds link to the original Coriolis fit and its guide.
- The Alliance Chieftain has a solo anti-xeno fit with four Guardian Gauss Cannons. The Panther Clipper Mk II has a shielded 1,168 t hauler and a 1,238 t carrier shuttle.

## Fleet carrier timers

- The overlay counts down to scheduled departure and shows an estimated cooldown after the jump. Arrival alerts require confirmation from the game journal. If only an indirect location update is available, the cooldown remains an estimate.

## Notifications

- **Settings → Configure notifications** opens 22 event settings. Enable or disable the whole system, mute all cues, or choose the overlay and sound separately for each event.
- Events cover carrier jumps and cooldown, low ship fuel, routes, trade rounds, exploration, construction, engineering and missions. Short sound cues are off by default.
