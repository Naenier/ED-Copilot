# ED Copilot 2.8.0

- Trading opens with profitable ship routes, with quotes, volumes and landing-pad requirements. Trade statistics now include daily and commodity totals, revenue, cost and realised margin.
- Carrier Trading suggests routes from available carrier capacity and market quotes. Compare purchases, jumps, tritium, return legs and costs, including options for Rackham’s Peak. The editable planner remains available.
- Colonisation, Mining, Engineering and Exobiology each have their own section. The colony overview lists every project. Construction shows resource bills and priorities; Supplies is a delivery log.
- Galaxy loads Politics, Community Goals and Powerplay automatically. Factions and conflicts appear when the selected system has observations.
- Frontier and Inara are in Settings. The sidebar holds the journal indicator, overlay switch and activity selector. Guide topics are grouped for reading.
