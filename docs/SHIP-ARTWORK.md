# Ship artwork

Assets borrowed from Elite Dangerous, with permission of Frontier Developments plc. ED Copilot is an independent, non-commercial fan project. Frontier Developments plc does not endorse it and had no involvement in its creation.

The color views come from Frontier's ship announcements, press material and store galleries. Each ship's original image and publication are recorded in [the photo source manifest](ship-photo-sources.json).

The ship plates pair those color views with recolored dorsal and ventral views maintained by the Elite Dangerous Almanac contributors. The underlying ship imagery and designs belong to Frontier Developments plc.
