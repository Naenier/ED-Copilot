# ED Copilot 2.8.8

- Pick an engineering blueprint before choosing a compatible module. The blueprint narrows the engineer list, and selecting a module enables the material calculation.
- Trade routes search across commodities by default. Turn on the commodity filter to select specific goods and set a minimum net profit for the whole trip, including the return leg if enabled.
- The colonization search shows eligible candidates only. The construction queue and its material requirements stay together, and deliveries are listed under **Supply log**.
- Standalone search fields now sit beside their headings, and exobiology sample cards have more space between them.
- Powerplay keeps the current table visible while loading the next page. Politics refreshes automatically while open; observation dates remain available.
- The encyclopedia now has a **Material farming** shortcut for raw, manufactured, encoded, and Guardian material guides.
