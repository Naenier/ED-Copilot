# ED Copilot 2.8.16

- Open **Galaxy → Surface coordinates** and enter a latitude and longitude to navigate to a point on a planet or moon.
- The Flight and Navigation overlay shows your current and target coordinates, compass course and surface distance when the game provides the body's radius.
- Guidance updates as the game reports a position in flight, in an SRV or on foot. Targets stay tied to the destination body and are saved in your profile.
