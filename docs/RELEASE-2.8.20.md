# ED Copilot 2.8.20

- Build ships in **Engineering → Ship builder**. Compare jump range, speed, power and combat stats as you change modules, engineering, fuel and cargo. Save and compare builds locally, import Coriolis builds and export them as JSON or SLEF.
- The ship builder totals materials for blueprints and experimental effects. See what you need, what you have and what is missing across raw materials, manufactured materials and encoded data.
- Use **Nearby search** to find stations, equipment, minor factions, star systems, bodies, signals and other points of interest. Each category has its own filters.
- The workspace opens while journals are read in the background. Larger sections load when you open them.
- Overview and the sidebar display the latest complete journal state, including your current system and navigation target.
