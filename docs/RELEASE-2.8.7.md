# ED Copilot 2.8.7

- Open **Galaxy → Systems** to find a system by name or browse nearby systems within 100 light-years. Filter results by population, allegiance, government, economy, and security.
- System pages show EDSM coordinates, permit requirements, celestial bodies and their materials, stations and services, and faction influence. Nearby search can fall back to Spansh and labels the provider. EDSM also supplements system name suggestions across the app.
- The engineering materials inventory now includes the exchange calculator. Engineer, module, blueprint, experimental effect, and material names in the progression plan link to their reference pages.
- Rewrote 49 module and 238 material articles. Material pages show collection conditions, trade ratios and links to higher-grade materials and recipes. The reference also covers 25 Odyssey modifications and the numerical effects of 68 ship experimental variants.
