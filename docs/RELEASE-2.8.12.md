# ED Copilot 2.8.12

- Added Road to Riches under Galaxy. Enter a starting system, ship jump range and search radius to plan a route through Earth-like worlds and water worlds. Each target shows its body name, distance from arrival, and estimated FSS and DSS payouts.
- The next system is copied to the clipboard when a route is ready and when the ship reaches a route stop. Overview and the flight overlay show the current target, next system, estimated payout and jump progress.
