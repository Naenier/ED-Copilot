# Colony development

ED Copilot's construction view compares the contributions of the buildings already completed with the additional effects of your queue. Select a building to see its population indices, security, technology, wealth, living standards, development, system score, landing pads, prerequisites, and potential station services. Set the primary port, the bodies and the exact settlement variants to improve the comparison.

The per-facility values are in-game indices transcribed from the measurements in [DaftMav's Stats sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1jsZOzNJSnPIWlU88puOc9gQXvsw-MGp8meoQ6k-Yj-Y/edit). The unweighted system scores and limited port/body population examples are based on [Silverback Research Institute's documented observations](https://svbkresearchinstitute.com/colonization). First-port weighting follows [Frontier's follow-up adjustment](https://forums.frontier.co.uk/threads/elite-dangerous-update-on-balancing-changes-to-system-development.643111/). Percentages apply to the signed contribution and do not change the separate system score.

Links and body economy overrides follow [Frontier's Trailblazers Update 3](https://store.steampowered.com/news/posts/?appids=359320&enddate=1746612179&feed=steam_community_announcements). The expected links use the facilities and body labels recorded in your plan, including the priority of larger ports and the transfer from planetary to orbital ports. Real market shares and services appear separately when the game journal reports them. Planetary population examples are reference observations for a small set of port and body combinations. The historical view uses observed population to calculate changes over time.

The workflow was compared with [Raven Colonial](https://ravencolonial.com/), [ED Colonise](https://edcolonise.net/) and [Elite Dangerous Colonisation Planner & Solver](https://gaborauth.github.io/ed-colonisation-planner/). The production calculation and interface are implemented in ED Copilot.
