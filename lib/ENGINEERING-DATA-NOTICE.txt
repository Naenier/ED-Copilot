Engineering data sources and attribution

Material IDs, symbols, types, trader categories and rarities:
EDCD/FDevIDs material.csv
https://github.com/EDCD/FDevIDs/blob/master/material.csv

Ship engineer IDs, system addresses and station market IDs:
EDCD/FDevIDs engineers.csv
https://github.com/EDCD/FDevIDs/blob/master/engineers.csv

Blueprint ingredients, experimental ingredients, module compatibility and
engineer grade capabilities:
EDCD/coriolis-data modifications/{blueprints,modules,specials}.json
https://github.com/EDCD/coriolis-data/tree/master/modifications

The Coriolis data license states:
"All Data and associated JSON files are intellectual property and copyright
of Frontier Developments plc ('Frontier', 'Frontier Developments') and are
subject to their terms and conditions."
https://github.com/EDCD/coriolis-data/blob/master/LICENSE.md
https://www.frontierstore.net/terms-and-conditions/

The MIT license for Coriolis code applies to its JavaScript, CSS, HTML and SVG
code, and is NOT asserted here as the license for Frontier's game data.

The ED Copilot implementation was independently written. No Coriolis or NEWP
application code, artwork or descriptive guide prose was copied into it.

The engineering planner feature comparison and factual ship engineer system
and base names were checked against NEWP on 2026-11-07:
https://newp.io/engineering#list
https://newp.io/engineers

Russian interface and reference labels were independently translated where
necessary. Existing official Russian material labels in the user's local game
journals were preferred. Only the material ID/name pair was retained; no
commander, account, location, time, inventory quantity or other journal content
is present in the reference data.

Journal events are interpreted using Frontier's Player Journal Manual v37:
https://hosting.zaonce.net/community/journal/v37/Journal_Manual_v37.pdf

The file engineering-data.json records its data capture date. Recipes and
capabilities describe ship engineering. Legacy experimental effects without
ingredient recipes are excluded, while costed modern equivalents are included.
The special CargoRack_IncreasedCapacity entry has no ingredient recipe or
crafting engineer in the source and is excluded from the regular crafting planner.
