ED Copilot encyclopedia data

The Russian explanatory articles and encyclopedia interface were independently
written for ED Copilot. No third-party guide prose or website UI was copied.

Ship names/IDs/symbols: EDCD/FDevIDs shipyard.csv
https://github.com/EDCD/FDevIDs/blob/master/shipyard.csv
Ship hull, pad class, crew, hardpoints and ordinary optional-slot data:
https://github.com/EDCD/coriolis-data/tree/master/ships
Snapshot fetched 2026-11-07: 49 ship identities, 44 with Coriolis specifications.
Missing specifications remain unknown.
